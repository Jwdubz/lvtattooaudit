What your site is costing you — LV Tattoo —

I'm Jarrett, your local Las Vegas Cinematic Web Designer. I went through lvtattoo.vegas the way a Fremont Street visitor would: find the shop, check the hours, book or walk in. The basics are there: a tap-to-call number, hours seven days a week, the address with a map, and artist pages. What stands out is weight and reliability: the home page loads about 12.6 MB over 152 requests, the server dropped connections several times during my checks, and the only video is a 2019 interview with one artist.

Checked October 17, 2026

What I found

01

The home page loads about 12.6 MB.

On two Lighthouse runs (a lab simulation of a phone on a slow connection) the home page transferred 12,558 KiB over 152 requests both times. The biggest files are two photos: LVtattoo_Cover1.jpg at 1,729 KiB and Tim.jpeg at 1,390 KiB. Lighthouse estimates about 4.9 MB could be saved with modern image formats, and 39 images load before they are needed. Largest Contentful Paint in the lab was 41.3 and 39.5 seconds.

02

The connection dropped several times during my checks.

In Lighthouse, 2 resources failed with ERR_CONNECTION_CLOSED. A full page load in Chrome failed once with the same error, a plain request for robots.txt failed once with an SSL error, and two of your links (/merch/ and /alyssa-m) timed out at 10 seconds, then loaded in 1 to 2.3 seconds on retry. I checked six other sites from the same machine the same way and they loaded normally, but I can't tell whether this is your server or my connection.

03

24 of 34 images have no description, and 6 links have no name.

Lighthouse also lists 14 low-contrast text spots and heading-order problems in 12 places. The links with no name include your logo and the Instagram and Facebook icons.

04

No search description, no share tags and no business markup.

The home page has no meta description, no Open Graph (link-preview) tags and no structured data. Its title is “LV Tattoo – Tattoo | Piercing”, and its only main heading is “LV TATTOO HOME”.

05

The only video is from 2019.

After the page runs in Chrome, it embeds one YouTube video: A chat with Tori of LV Tattoo in Las Vegas, 46 seconds, about 1,600 views. It is set not to autoplay. Your TikTok, @lvtattooandpiercing, has the same handle as your linked Instagram and Facebook, and its latest clip was posted on October 17. The Fremont and chair-side clips on it are not on the site.

06

At phone width in a desktop browser, the page is 10 px too wide.

In a desktop browser window set to 390 px wide, the page measured 400 px, so it scrolls sideways a little. I haven't tested on a phone, so this is a pointer, not a verdict.
